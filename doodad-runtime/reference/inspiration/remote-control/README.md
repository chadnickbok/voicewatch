Google's first-party Wear OS camera guidance confirms the center shutter,
three-second timer, zoom slider, and camera-switch behavior:
[Control your phone's camera with your watch](https://support.google.com/wearos/answer/11532572).

The oracle takes the references' hierarchy rather than their round geometry:
the live viewfinder owns the surface, connection/timer state is compact and
overlaid, and shutter/recovery actions remain reachable without a title bar.
The packaged desert viewfinder is an original generated fixture, not one of
these research images.
